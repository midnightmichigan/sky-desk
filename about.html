ABOUT THE SKY DESK

The Sky Desk is the website companion to The Sky Desk Facebook page — a real-time UFO and UAP news desk.

We publish the latest civilian sighting reports (sourced from public reporting databases like UFO Stalker's open API), keep the full paper trail of official U.S. government UAP releases in one place, and tell you exactly how to file your own report.

We don't fabricate reports, and we don't hype. Witness summaries are printed as reported. Official documents are linked to the originals. What the data says, we print — what it doesn't, we say plainly.

AARO FY2024 Consolidated Annual Report on UAP

The Pentagon's All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office received 757 UAP reports in the period May 2023–June 2024. 118 cases resolved as balloons, birds, and drones; no evidence of extraterrestrial beings, activity, or technology found.

Source: https://www.uap-blog.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/FY24-CONSOLIDATED-ANNUAL-REPORT-ON-UAP-508_FR.pdf
(PDF mirror of the official DoD report (via uap-blog.com))

AARO FY2023 Consolidated Annual Report on UAP

The FY2023 edition covering UAP reports through August 2023 — the report that formalized the government's UAP definitions and reporting trends.

Source: https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/UNCLASSIFIED-FY23_Consolidated_Annual_Report_on_UAP-Oct_25_2023_1236.pdf

NASA UAP Independent Study Team Report (Sept 2023)

NASA's 33-page report: more science and less stigma needed to understand UAPs. Found no evidence of extraterrestrial origin; recommended systematic data collection using NASA's Earth-observing fleet.

Source: https://uap-archive.org/uap/docs/nasa-uap-independent-study-team-report.pdf
(PDF via uap-archive.org)

ODNI Preliminary Assessment on UAP (June 2021)

The landmark 9-page assessment of 144 UAP encounters reported by US government sources between 2004 and 2021 — 80 captured on multiple sensors, one the only one explained.

Source: https://www.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/Prelimary-Assessment-UAP-20210625.pdf
(Official PDF, dni.gov)

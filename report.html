REPORT A SIGHTING

Saw something you can't explain? File it with the two big civilian databases — both are free and take reports worldwide.

  1. NUFORC — National UFO Reporting Center (nuforc.org). The longest-running civilian reporting center, active since 1974. Use their online report form — include date, time, exact location, duration, direction of travel, and what the object did. Reports feed their public recent-reports index.
  2. MUFON — Mutual UFO Network (mufon.com). The largest civilian UFO investigation organization. File through their case management system — trained field investigators may follow up on compelling cases.
  3. UFO Stalker (ufostalker.com). Community map of recent sightings with photos and videos — great for checking whether anyone else saw what you saw.

Tips for a useful report: note the exact time and your GPS location, estimate the object's angular size (fingertip at arm's length covers ~1°), record video if you can do so safely, and never chase or approach an unknown object.
